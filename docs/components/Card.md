# Card

A content block on `svr-color-bg` with a `svr-color-border` outline and a `svr-color-accent` rule across the top, headed in Bebas Neue.

**Provide:** `title`, optional `eyebrow`, `children` (body copy), optional `footer` (usually a small secondary Button). The consumer provides the grid that lays cards out.

- Card headers use `svr-subheading-h2` in `svr-color-heading`, per the guidelines' H2 role.
- Keep body copy to two or three sentences.
- Place cards on the page or in `default`, `alt` and `inverse` bands, never inside a `red` band.
